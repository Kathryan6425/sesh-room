About The Sesh Room

What is this? A cozy corner of the internet for cannabis folks — a friendly feed, six chat rooms, and live video seshes. Like the group chat that never sleeps. Made by Geeksmith Made Apps.

How it works: pick a display name (tap "Set your name" up top — it can be anything friendly), post to the feed, hop into a room, or join a live sesh. No email, no password.

House rules 🏠

  • 21+ only. Adults, please.
  • Be kind. Disagree without being a jerk.
  • No buying or selling. Talk strains all day; don't deal here.
  • Keep it legal where you live, and don't drive high. Ever.
  • No spam. One post says it fine.

This is a small community project. Names are display names only — don't share private info you'd mind the room seeing.

What should the room call you?

Any friendly display name — it's how your posts and messages will show up.
